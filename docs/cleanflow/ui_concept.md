# CleanFlow UI/UX Concept

A complete visual and interaction concept for the CleanFlow app — inspired by the smooth, delightful experience of **Monobank**, but optimized for calm productivity and adaptive cleaning management.

---

## 🧭 1. Overall Style & Feel

**Design Language:**
- Minimalist, calm, and elegant.
- Rounded cards, soft gradients, airy spacing.
- Accent color: soft teal or pastel mint.
- Base: off-white and light gray neutrals.
- Typography: Inter or SF Pro.
- Motion: subtle, smooth transitions (no bounce).
- Positive tone — no guilt colors or warnings.

---

## 🏠 2. Home Screen — “Today’s Plan”

### Layout
- **Header:** Greeting + progress ring.
- **Main Card:** “🧹 Today’s Plan”
  - “Now”: one focus task.
  - “Next up”: 3–4 tasks.
  - “Later”: optional list.
- **Quick Mode Selector:**
  - ⚡ Focus, 🌙 Low Energy, 🧼 Full Reset, 🐾 Pet Mode.
- Task chips: emoji, title, estimated time, and status.

### Interactions
- Swipe right → Done ✅
- Swipe left → Skip ⏭️
- Tap → Task detail
- Long press → Edit or assign

Micro-animation: soft confetti when a task is done.

---

## 🧩 3. Task Detail View

- Task name, icon, and estimated time.
- Buttons: Done, Skip, Postpone.
- Comments bubble with presets.
- “Mark & Next” for fast flow.
- QR icon (if printed task linked).

**Motion:** smooth slides, haptic feedback, soft chime.

---

## 🗓️ 4. Planner Screen

Tabs: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Seasonal**, **Custom**.

### Layout
- Scrollable timeline grouped by date.
- Task cards per room.
- Floating Action Button: “+ Custom Task”.

Modal for adding a task includes:
- Task name
- Room
- Frequency
- Assignee
- Save

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 5. Family Mode

- Top toggle: “My Plan” / “Family Plan”.
- Horizontal avatars as filters.
- Central progress ring (family completion %).
- Color-coded cards for each person.
- “Fairness” chart for workload balance.
- 🖨️ Print button for family plan.

---

## 🧒 6. Kids / Single Room Mode

- Large, colorful emoji cards.
- Voice guidance + success sounds.
- Big “Next” button with tactile animation.
- “Print this plan” option with big checkboxes + QR.

---

## 🧠 7. Insights & AI Screen

- Weekly stats: completion %, total time, streaks.
- Smart suggestions cards (“Adjust schedule?”, “Merge tasks?”).
- Accept / Dismiss actions.

Tone: gentle, encouraging, friendly.

---

## 🖨️ 8. Paper Bridge

- Button: “🖨️ Generate PDF”.
- Options: Daily/Weekly, QR codes on/off, layout style.
- Preview thumbnail of printable sheet.
- Scanning a QR → opens mobile form for quick sync.

---

## ⚙️ 9. Settings & Customization

Sections:
- Home Setup
- Appearance
- Reminders
- AI Preferences
- Account & Family

Optional “Paper-first workflow” toggle.

---

## ✨ 11. Transitions & Animations

- Page transitions: soft slide.
- Task done: checkmark animation.
- Mode change: color hue shift.
- Loading: calm ripple effect.
- Sound cues: soft “click” or “sweep”.
